  confirm that MIGs provide autohealing, autoscaling, rolling updates, and

  confirms proactive updates, surge/unavailable controls, stability checks,
  and rollback by applying the earlier instance template.
- [Google autohealing](https://docs.cloud.google.com/compute/docs/instance-groups/autohealing-instances-in-migs)

  confirms CPU, load-balancing, and per-instance metrics cannot scale to zero;

  distinguishes whole-group work metrics from metrics emitted by each VM.
